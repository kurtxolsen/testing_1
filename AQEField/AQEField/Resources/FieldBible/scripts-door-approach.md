# Door Approach Scripts

## The Opener (storm-hit neighborhood)

"Hey, sorry to bother you — I'll be quick. I'm Kurt with American Quality
Exteriors. We're doing inspections on this street because of the hail that
came through on [DATE]. Your neighbors at [ADDRESS/`the gray house on the
corner`] just had us up on their roof. Have you had anyone take a look at
yours yet?"

**Why it works:** apology + brevity promise lowers the guard; the specific
storm date and neighbor reference prove you're not cold-canvassing; the
question ends on *their* roof, not your pitch.

## If they hesitate

"Totally fair. Here's the thing — hail damage isn't usually visible from the
ground, and there's a filing window with your insurance. The inspection is
free, takes about 15 minutes, and if there's nothing up there I'll tell you
that and you'll have it in writing for peace of mind. Worst case, you get a
free roof checkup."

## The two-option close (never yes/no)

"I've got time right now, or I'm back in this neighborhood Thursday
afternoon — which works better for you?"

## Leaving a no-answer

Door hanger + note the house in the app as **No Answer**. Best re-knock
windows: weekday 5–7pm, Saturday 10am–1pm.

## Rules

- Smile before the door opens — they see you through the peephole first.
- Step BACK off the porch after knocking. Space = safety = longer listen.
- First 7 seconds decide the conversation. Name, company, reason, neighbor.
- Never say "free roof." Say "free inspection" and "insurance-covered
  replacement if it qualifies."
