# Carrier Intel (Adjuster Meeting Prep)

Generalized industry patterns — starting hypotheses, not gospel. Update
after every adjuster meeting (`adjprep learn` keeps the master file).

## State Farm

**Pushback:** disputes hail size vs. NOAA; pushes repair-over-replace under
8 hits/square; calls wind creases "mechanical damage."
**Counter:** 10x10 test squares on EVERY slope, chalk-circle each impact;
shoot soft metals first (independent hail-size proof); cite manufacturer
repairability guidelines on brittle shingles.

## Allstate

**Pushback:** repair scopes for "scattered" damage; desk review cuts code
items; aggressive depreciation holdbacks on older roofs.
**Counter:** chalk + photograph every impact so scattered reads systemic;
attach local code citation for every code item; confirm RCV vs ACV language
and note recoverable depreciation before the meeting.

## Farmers

**Pushback:** sends ladder-assist / third-party inspectors; reclassifies
bruising as blistering; partial (one-slope) approvals on directional storms.
**Counter:** meet EVERY inspection in person; break a bruise open on video
(fractured mat = impact, blisters have no bruise); document collateral on
all elevations for multi-directional impact.

## USAA

**Pushback:** fair but documentation-heavy; slow desk-review supplements;
questions discontinued-shingle matching without ITEL.
**Counter:** over-document the FIRST submission (full photo report, test
squares, measurements); submit supplements complete-in-one; order the ITEL
report early.

## Travelers

**Pushback:** engineer reports to dispute causation; denies creases as
thermal cracking/foot traffic; omits steep/high and tear-off quantities.
**Counter:** be present when the engineer walks, photograph what they
photograph; match crease directionality to the NOAA wind vector; verify
pitch/layers/access yourself before their scope lands.

## Liberty Mutual

**Pushback:** slow cycle times; steers to preferred-vendor repair programs;
betterment/wear arguments on soft metals.
**Counter:** follow-up touch every 3 business days, confirm calls in
writing; remind the homeowner THEY choose the contractor; scale-reference
photos on soft metals — dents are impact, not wear.
