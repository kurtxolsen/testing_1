# Roof Anatomy (Field Reference)

Know every part you're pointing at. Bottom-up:

## Structure

- **Decking / sheathing** — plywood or OSB the roof is built on. Rot or
  delamination here = line item, not shingle work.
- **Drip edge** — metal at eaves/rakes directing water off the fascia.
  Code-required in most jurisdictions → supplement if the scope omits it.
- **Underlayment** — felt or synthetic moisture barrier over the deck.
- **Ice & water shield** — self-adhering membrane at eaves, valleys,
  penetrations. Code item in freeze climates.

## Shingle courses

- **Starter strip** — first course at the eave, adhesive band out. Missing
  starter = wind claims waiting to happen.
- **Field shingles** — the main coverage. Architectural (laminated) vs
  3-tab (flat, single-layer, mostly legacy).
- **Hip & ridge caps** — folded shingles finishing the peaks.

## Flashing (where roofs actually leak)

- **Step flashing** — L-shaped pieces woven into courses along walls.
- **Counter/apron flashing** — covers step flashing at chimneys/walls.
- **Valley flashing** — open metal valleys vs closed-cut shingle valleys.
- **Pipe boots** — neoprene cracks by year 10–12; cheapest leak source.

## Ventilation (the warranty keeper)

- **Intake:** soffit vents. **Exhaust:** ridge vent / box vents / turbines.
- Rule of thumb: 1 sq ft net free area per 300 sq ft attic, balanced
  intake/exhaust. Starved ventilation cooks shingles and voids warranties.

## Measurement language

- **Square** = 100 sq ft. Typical home: 25–35 squares.
- **Pitch** = rise/run ("6/12"). 7/12+ earns steep charges; 2-story+ earns
  high charges. Verify both before the adjuster's scope lands.
- **Waste factor** — hips/valleys add cut waste; 10–15% typical.
