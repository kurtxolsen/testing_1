# The GAF Roofing System

Why we lead with GAF, in homeowner language.

## The pitch

"We install GAF — North America's largest shingle manufacturer. What matters
to you isn't the shingle, it's the system: when the whole roof is GAF
components installed by a GAF-certified contractor, the factory warranty
covers the entire roof as a system, not just the shingles."

## The system layers (what's in the quote)

1. **Roof deck protection** — synthetic underlayment (e.g. Deck-Armor™),
   the moisture barrier over the decking.
2. **Leak barrier** — ice & water shield (WeatherWatch™/StormGuard™) at
   eaves, valleys, and penetrations. Code-required in cold climates.
3. **Starter strips** (Pro-Start™/WeatherBlocker™) — factory-adhesive edge
   course that resists wind lift where blow-offs begin.
4. **Field shingles** — Timberline HDZ® with LayerLock™ technology and the
   StrikeZone™ nailing area.
5. **Attic ventilation** (Cobra®) — heat/moisture exhaust that protects the
   shingles from cooking from underneath and keeps the warranty valid.
6. **Ridge caps** (Seal-A-Ridge®/TimberTex®) — finished hip & ridge.

## Timberline HDZ talking points

- Best-selling shingle in North America — not an experiment.
- LayerLock bond powers the **WindProven™ limited wind warranty: no maximum
  wind speed limit** when installed with the required combination of GAF
  accessories (first of its kind).
- StrikeZone nailing area = faster, more accurate fastening = fewer
  installer errors, which is what actually causes most roof failures.

## Certification tiers (why the contractor matters)

- **GAF Certified™ / Master Elite®** contractors can register enhanced
  warranties. Master Elite is roughly the top few percent of contractors.
- Enhanced warranties (System Plus → Silver Pledge → **Golden Pledge®**)
  add years of workmanship coverage backed by GAF itself — see the
  Warranty Guide article for the tiers.
