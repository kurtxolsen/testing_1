# Follow-Up SOP (Roofing Strong Cadence)

A lead without a follow-up plan is a donation to the next rep. The app's
Follow-Up engine runs this cadence automatically once a lead is logged.

## The cadence

| Touch | When | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Same day | Text | Thank + recap + photo of THEIR roof |
| 2 | Day 1 | Call | Answer questions, set/confirm inspection |
| 3 | Day 3 | Text | Value drop (neighbor approval, storm map) |
| 4 | Day 7 | Call + VM | Direct ask for the appointment |
| 5 | Day 14 | Text | Deadline frame (filing window) |
| 6 | Day 30 | Call | Last direct attempt, ask permission to check quarterly |
| 7+ | Quarterly | Text | Stay top-of-mind until roof is replaced (by someone) |

## Rules

- **Speed to lead:** touch 1 within the hour beats touch 1 same-evening.
- **Every touch adds value.** Never "just checking in" — bring a reason:
  a neighbor's approval, an adjuster date on the street, a storm update.
- **Two channels minimum.** Some people never answer calls; some never
  read texts. Alternate.
- **Log every touch** the moment it happens (the app timestamps it).
- **Overdue = today's first job.** Red alerts in Reports clear before new
  knocking starts.

## Status ladder

New → Contacted → Inspection Set → Inspected → Claim Filed →
Adjuster Met → Approved → Contracted → Built → Closed
(Dead and Denied are exits at any rung; a Denied with a reinspection
right is a lead, not a loss.)

## Text templates

**Touch 1:** "Kurt w/ AQE — great meeting you. Here's the photo of your
[N] elevation I mentioned. I'm holding [DAY] at [TIME] for your
inspection — does that still work?"

**Touch 5:** "Heads up — claims from the [DATE] storm need to be filed
inside the policy window. Takes 15 min to document your roof either way.
Want me to swing by this week?"
