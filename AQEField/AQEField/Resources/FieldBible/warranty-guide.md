# Warranty Guide (GAF Tiers)

What each tier actually covers — and the homeowner sentence for each.

## Standard limited warranty (any installer)

Materials only, prorated after an initial period. Covers factory defects —
which cause almost no real-world failures. Workmanship (the actual failure
cause) is on the installer's own promise.

## System Plus (GAF Certified™ & up)

Lifetime material coverage on the full system + extended non-prorated
window. "The factory stands behind every component, not just shingles."

## Silver Pledge™ (Master Elite® only)

Adds **10-year workmanship coverage backed by GAF** — if the installer
disappears, GAF still honors it.

## Golden Pledge® (Master Elite® only)

The flagship: lifetime material + **25-year workmanship coverage backed by
GAF**, with a GAF inspection of the finished roof.

Homeowner sentence: "Even if we closed our doors tomorrow, GAF itself
warranties the workmanship on your roof for 25 years — that's coverage a
guy-with-a-truck can't offer at any price."

## Selling with warranties

- Warranty tier = contractor credential. Only Master Elite can register
  the top tiers — that's the differentiation, not price.
- Ventilation requirements apply — an unvented or starved attic can void
  coverage. (Our ventilation line item isn't an upsell; it's warranty
  protection.)
- Warranties transfer once within the early ownership window — a real
  resale-value point for homeowners planning to move.
